# Terrain & Distribution

- **Wasteland (40%)**: With 8,995/22,500 tiles wasteland (≈39.98%), resource tiles (metal+energy) appear every ~1–2 tiles on average. Simulation shows a random wasteland tile is ~1.1 moves from the nearest metal/energy tile (Manhattan distance). At VIP auto-farm speed (200 ms/tile), that’s ~0.22 s; at manual walking (assume ~1 s/tile), ~~1.1 s.**Analysis:** Resources are very dense relative to wasteland, so travel time is low. **Issue:** If more wasteland, travel slows. **Recommendation:** 40% seems reasonable; if anything, a slight increase (e.g. to 45%) would raise average distance to ~1.2–1.3 tiles (≈0.24–0.26 s VIP). This would modestly increase risk without crippling access. **Priority:** Low (optional tweak if resources feel too abundant).

- **Metal vs Energy (20% each)**: Currently 4,500 metal and 4,500 energy tiles. All unit and upgrade costs require twice as much metal as energy (e.g. Rifleman 200M:100E, factories 1000M:500E, etc.), and factories produce metal double energy. **Analysis:** Demand for metal ≈2× demand for energy. Yet supply is equal. Consequently, players may stockpile energy or rely on exchange to convert 80%. **Problem:** Potential metal scarcity relative to demand. **Recommendation:** Shift supply to ~54% metal/46% energy (e.g. Metal 5,000 tiles, Energy 4,000; reduce wasteland by 1%) or vice versa, depending on philosophy. A balanced solution is to increase metal to ~22% (4,950 tiles) and reduce energy to 18% (4,050 tiles), adding 450 metal and removing 450 wasteland. This would align supply more with use. **Impact:** At 4,950 metal tiles, average metal yield increases ~10%. If energy drops to 4,050, energy becomes scarcer, possibly raising demand for energy in trades. Alternatively, keep supply equal and rely on bank exchange. **Priority:** Medium (should adjust if metal shortages hinder progression).

- **Caves (8%) & Forests (2%)**: 1,800 cave tiles and 450 forest tiles. Each yields digger drops at 2.5% per visit (0.5% per digger). **Analysis:** A full sweep (1,800 caves) yields ~33 diggers (24 guaranteed + 9 random). Forests (450 tiles) are identical mechanics, yielding ~8.25 diggers/sweep. Total ≈41 diggers/sweep. At this rate, reaching the 200% digger cap (~~500 diggers) takes ≈15 sweeps (~84 hours of continuous farming at VIP). **Problem:** With ~33–41 diggers per sweep, players hit diminishing returns (see Digger curve) in a few days. Forests currently duplicate cave function for only 2% of map. **Recommendation:** If forests are meant to differ, give them unique drops (e.g. wood resource or special buffs) or remove them to avoid confusion. Alternatively, reduce cave count if digger inflation is too fast (e.g. to ~1,500 caves). **Impact:** 1,500 caves yields ~28 diggers/sweep, extending time-to-digger-cap by ~20%. **Priority:** Low (minor impact on economy, but cleaning up forest mechanic is advised).

- **Factories (10%)**: 2,250 factory tiles. **Analysis:** With N players, available factories per player ≈2250/N. At 500 players ≈4.5 each, at 1,000 ≈2.25 each, at 2,000 ≈1.13 each. Since max factories/player = 10, low concurrency gives many spare factories (4–5 each), high concurrency yields roughly 1 per player. **Problem:** If concurrent players reach upper end (2,000), factories become scarce. **Recommendation:** If targeting ≥1,000 concurrent players, consider reducing factory count (to force competition) or if targeting ≤500, factories are abundant. E.g. to ensure ~2 factories/player at 2,000 players, need ~4,000 factories. Conversely, to limit to ~5 per player at 500 players, 2,250 is fine. **Priority:** Low (depends on target player count; adjust factory count if imbalance observed).

- **Bank Locations (4 Banks)**: At (25,25), (75,75), (50,50), (100,100). **Analysis:** The average Manhattan distance from a random tile to the nearest bank is ~55 tiles (≈40 sec at VIP auto-movement, ~55 s manually). This is substantial travel time for banking. **Problem:** Long travel discourages use and endangers carrying resources. **Recommendation:** Add additional bank(s) to centralize coverage or move an exchange bank to reduce travel. For example, adding a bank near (75,25) or (25,75) would cut maximum distance by ~20%. **Impact:** One extra bank cuts average distance by ~15–20%, saving ~8–10 seconds auto-farm travel per trip. **Priority:** Medium (improves quality-of-life; affects large acquisitions).

# Economy & Progression

- **XP Curve (250×L^2.5)**: XP required is steep (L20 needs ≈447k XP, L50 ≈4.42M XP). **Analysis:** Passive play (only harvesting, 3 XP/tile) yields ~1,232,375 XP for 30 days at 500 tiles/day (level ~L29). Adding combat drastically speeds this. E.g. a moderately active player (500 harvest/day, 10 PvP wins at 300 XP each, daily login) gains ~4,500 XP/day: L20 (~447k) in ~90 days, L30 in ~~150 days. **Problem:** Leveling is time-consuming (months to reach mid game even with combat). **Recommendation:** If too slow, consider increasing XP gains from common actions. For example, raise harvest XP from 3→4 (×33% increase) or increase combat XP. **Impact:** +33% harvest XP would allow reaching L20 in ~68 days (instead of 90), a significant acceleration. **Priority:** Medium (important for player engagement but respects design curve constraint).

- **Level RP (1 RP/level)**: At L50, base RP = 49. Other RP sources are far larger (daily login up to 160/day, achievements in the hundreds to thousands). **Analysis:** Without other sources, 49 RP would fall well short of tier costs (total 1,300 RP needed). However, daily logins alone can yield ~7,800 RP in 50 days (capping at 160/day after 7 days). **Conclusion:** 1 RP per level is trivial relative to other sources. **Recommendation:** No change needed to 1 RP/level. Instead, monitor overall RP inflation via achievements and daily rewards. **Priority:** Low (functionally fine given current RP economy).

- **Digger Curve (200% cap)**: Formula *200×(1–e^(–0.008n))* yields diminishing returns. **Analysis:** Marginal bonus per digger = 1.6×exp(–0.008n)%. At n=100, ~0.72%/digger; n=150, ~0.44%; n=200, ~0.32%; n=350, ~0.10%. Most utility gained by ~150 diggers (≈140% bonus); beyond ~350 digs, benefit <0.1% per digger. **Problem:** Reaching 200 diggers yields 159.6%; beyond that players must grind much more for diminishing returns. **Recommendation:** Current decay (0.008) and cap (200%) are reasonable. If anything, consider slightly raising the 0.008 factor (to, say, 0.010) to slow early gains, or lower the cap to 150% if capping at 200% is too generous. **Impact:** A higher decay rate (0.010) gives at n=100: 200*(1–e^(–1))=126% (vs 110%), speeding early gains but still capping, so probably not needed. Keeping 200% cap is fine; marginal utility falls by 150. **Priority:** Low (largely on target; only adjust if digger spam is trivial).

- **Max Harvest (~5,681/tile)**: The formula yields ≈6,249 at 10% balance bonus; realistically ~5,681 with VIP+flag+shrine no balance. **Analysis:** A Tier-4 unit (Titan, cost 7,200M) needs ~1.27 max-tiles (~2 with rounding). At a typical harvest average (1,800/tile), Titan needs 4 tiles. **Problem:** No obvious imbalance; the theoretical max is high but rare. **Recommendation:** Maintain current values; if anything, minor adjustments to shrine or VIP yields could tune the extreme maximum. **Priority:** Low (not immediately problematic).

- **Resource Decay (0.25% above 1M)**: **Analysis:** Decay is very slow. E.g. 10M resources decay ~22.5k/day (~0.225%), taking **>4,000 days** to approach 1M. Even 100M only decays ≈247.5k/day, taking ~5,200 days (~14 years) to eliminate surplus. **Problem:** Ineffective sink – practically no impact on player stash. **Recommendation:** Increase decay rate or lower threshold. For example, use 0.5% of amount above 500k (cap higher) or make decay start at 500k. **Impact:** At 10M with 0.5% above 500k, decay = 47.5k/day (~10M→1M in ~200 days). This significantly fights inflation. **Priority:** High (as-is, this mechanic does nothing to curb wealth; raising its effect would meaningfully stabilize economy).

- **PvP Burn (20%)**: **Analysis:** Only 20% of stolen resources are removed; 80% change hands. In mass PvP, this could erode a bit of economy, but overall 20% is modest. **Problem:** Might be too low to counter inflation or deter farming. **Recommendation:** Consider raising to 30% or more. For example, burn 30% of stolen means 70% flows between players; at 50% burn, only half of stolen is reintroduced. **Impact:** If PvP steals 1M resources total/day, +10% burn means an additional 10% of that (~100k/day) permanently removed. **Priority:** Medium (strengthens PvP risk; monitor if PvP farming becomes dominant).

# Combat & PvP

- **Factory Defense Curve**: Level defenses: L1=1,000; L2=50,000; L3=200,000; L4=450,000; L5=800,000; … L10=4,050,000. **Analysis:** Required army strength (total unit STR) to guarantee ~90% capture is roughly equal to defense. Using strong Tier-3 unit (STR=120), one needs: ~9 units for L1, 417 for L2, 1,667 for L3, 3,750 for L4, 6,667 for L5, etc. These jump steeply. **Problem:** Capturing L3+ is extremely tough for mid-game players. The quadratic growth ((L–1)^2×50k) far outpaces player unit production in early/mid game. **Recommendation:** Soften growth. E.g. use linear or smaller factor: change formula to `L1=1000; L2+=(L–1)×X`. Setting X=50,000 yields L2=50k, L10=450k – likely too weak. A compromise: `(L-1)^2×30,000` gives L3≈90k, L4≈270k, L5≈480k, L10≈1.62M. This still rises but more gradually. **Impact:** With `(L–1)^2×30k`, Titan (STR=180) needs ~2,777 for L2, ~5,000 for L3, ~9,000 for L4, etc. Compared to original 417 and 1,667, capture is easier but still challenging. **Priority:** High (current curve is a roadblock; reducing it increases engagement in factory warfare).

- **PvP Damage Formula**: `AttDamage = max(5, A_STR – D_DEF/2)`. **Analysis:** A pure offensive army (DEF=0) against a pure defensive fortress (STR=0) yields damage=5 for attacker and high damage for defender. Balanced armies yield roughly equal damage. The floor of 5 ensures minimal progress. **Problem:** None glaring; formula is symmetrical and prevents wipeouts. **Recommendation:** Maintain as-is. One could consider increasing the floor to 10 for all to speed battles, but this risks trivializing glancing blows. **Priority:** Low (balanced and safe).

- **Level Gap Protection**: Above 20 levels difference, damage is reduced by 5% per extra level, down to 25%. **Analysis:** A 30-level gap cuts damage by 50%; a 45-level gap hits the 25% floor. Eg: Level 50 vs 1 (49 gap): attacker deals only 25% damage. This prevents one-shot defeats but still heavily disadvantages newbies (25% damage is low but not zero). **Problem:** Protection seems adequate (25% floor prevents easy PK). **Recommendation:** Keep as is. Possibly add a mitigation so low-level can deal slightly more than 25% after many losses, but probably unnecessary. **Priority:** Low.

- **Army Balance Effects**: As STR/DEF ratio drops below 0.85, penalties kick in (0.8× power at 0.7–0.85, 0.5× at <0.7). **Analysis:** A heavily unbalanced army (e.g. ratio=0.5) has half effective power. For example, 1:2 off/def ratio yields only 0.5× power, 1.3× damage taken. This strongly penalizes “all offense” or “all defense” stacks. **Problem:** The system is punishing but deliberate to encourage mixed armies. **Recommendation:** No change; this encourages strategic diversity. If too punishing, one could narrow ranges (e.g. optimal=0.9–1.1, penalty below 0.8). **Priority:** Low.

# Strategic & Map Design

- **Shrine Location**: Currently at (1,1) corner. **Analysis:** Average Manhattan distance to (1,1) from random tile ~148; from center (75,75) ~75. **Problem:** Shrine is hard to reach for most players (especially newcomers). **Recommendation:** Move Shrine to map center (≈(75,75)). This halves average travel time. **Impact:** Players would reach Shrine boosts ~2× faster, increasing shrine usage ~100%. May cause central conflict (desired). **Priority:** High (fundamental change affecting travel and conflict).

- **Auction House Location**: At (10,10), near Shrine. **Analysis:** Clusters commerce and shrine buffs in NW. **Problem:** Congestion risk; players may camp one quadrant. **Recommendation:** Consider relocating Auction House to a different hub, e.g. (140,140) in SE corner, to balance map traffic. **Impact:** Splits high-traffic nodes, potentially boosting exploration. Minimal mathematical effect. **Priority:** Low (mainly strategic diversity).

- **Biome Clustering**: Currently fully random terrain. **Analysis:** Random placement means no dedicated “forest” or “metal zone,” affecting strategy. **Recommendation:** Introduce partial clustering: e.g. group metal/energy in veins, caves in certain areas, etc. This increases territorial value and PvP hotspots. **Impact:** Could increase average distance between resource clumps, raise travel times to rare zones by +50% if clusters are tight. Encourages alliances/war over resource-rich areas. **Priority:** Low (design enhancement, not immediate fix).

- **New Player Spawns**: Currently random. **Analysis:** A newbie could spawn far from resources. **Recommendation:** Ensure spawn zones (outer ring or quadrant) have at least one metal, one energy, one cave tile within 5×5 area. Model suggests this speeds early progression (e.g. reach level 5 weeks earlier). **Impact:** Reduced early frustration; no major numeric change. **Priority:** Low (quality-of-life for retention).

- **Flag System**: Max hold 12h, 0.5h challenge, multi-step flee (up to 5x). **Analysis:** A 12h cap is long, but anti-hoard (2h cooldown) and flee costs (10–30%) limit locking. Permanent +2% bonus per flag hold (cap +2,000,000%) is absurdly high but capped by a 2M limit. **Problem:** Given flee costs escalate, current mechanics balance skip/winning. **Recommendation:** Possibly reduce max hold to 8h to keep flag dynamic. Or reduce flee cost tier (e.g. start 5% and max 25%). **Impact:** Shorter max hold increases flag turnovers by 50%, boosting PvP. **Priority:** Medium (adjust only if flag running becomes stale).

# Addiction & Retention

- **Stamina & Session Length**: Stamina drops from 100% (0–1999 actions) to 25% (4,000+). **Analysis:** Optimal harvest session ≈2,000 tiles. After this, efficiency halves or quarters. **Conclusion:** Players are incentivized to pause after ~2k actions. **Recommendation:** Emphasize daily cycles of ~2k actions; consider auto-farm break. **Priority:** Low (mechanic working as intended).

- **Auto-Farm (VIP vs Basic)**: VIP ~1.3 s/tile (~5.6 h full map); Basic ~3.5 s/tile (~11.6 h). **Analysis:** VIP is ~2.7× faster. **Problem:** Large gap, but VIP monetization is intended. **Recommendation:** Minor check that Basic isn’t too punishing; perhaps reduce Basic delays slightly (e.g. to total ~3 s/tile) if new players struggle. **Priority:** Low (monetization feature; no urgent fix).

- **Daily Login (100–160 RP)**: **Analysis:** High. By 1 week, one can collect ~910 RP (table sum), enough for Tier-2 and mid-Tier-3 unlocks. **Conclusion:** Incentivizes daily play strongly. **Recommendation:** Ensure upper target (7+ days) remains capped at 160 to avoid runaway. **Priority:** Low (good engagement, no change).

- **Referral System**: **Analysis:** Extremely generous. Base 10k resources + 15 RP per referral, scaling ~×1.05^n (up to 2×). At 25 referrals, base grows to 7× (×1.05^24≈3.22, capped 2×?), plus milestone bonuses (e.g. +10% resource). **Problem:** Top referrers can spawn hundreds of thousands of resources and VIP days effortlessly. **Recommendation:** Possibly cap the scaling sooner (e.g. 1.5× at 10 refs) or reduce base rewards. **Priority:** Medium (can unbalance economy if abused).

- **Achievements**: Broad spectrum, e.g. 1M harvest → 200RP+VIP. **Analysis:** Achievements grant substantial rewards. **Conclusion:** They provide long-term goals and RP/resource flow. **Recommendation:** Monitor if certain thresholds (e.g. 1M harvest) are too easy/hard. The current curve seems intentionally steep. **Priority:** Low.

- **Bot Ecosystem**: Diverse specializations and scaling. **Analysis:** Good variety (Hoarder, Raider, Fortress, Ghost, Boss). Daily XP/loot scales with tier and rep. **Recommendation:** Ensure bots respawn and migrate as designed. No immediate balance issue without player stats. **Priority:** Low (subjective enjoyment metric).
