## Target Spec — Complete Feature List

### 1. Session Earnings Tracking
- Migration: `players.flag_session_metal`, `flag_session_energy`, `flag_session_started_at`
- Tracks only resources earned WHILE holding flag, reset on transfer

### 2. Keep & Pay / Flee Mechanic
- Progressive: 1st=10%, 2nd=15%, 3rd=20%, 4th=25%, 5th=30%
- Max 5 flees. 6th = auto-lose. Bearer teleports 5 tiles random.
- Payment to challenger. Flee cooldown: 60s.

### 3. 30-Second Challenge System
- Replaces direct combat. No HP, no damage.
- 30s channel. Both players locked 5s. Bearer unlocked → choose FLEE or WAIT.
- After 30s: if bearer hasn't fled → flag transfers.
- Queue: first-come-first-served. Cancellable (5-min cooldown).

### 4. Grace Period & Time Limits
- 1hr grace after transfer. 12hr max hold with warnings. Auto-drop → 30min respawn.
- +2% permanent harvest bonus per 12hr completion.

### 5. Restrictions While Holding
- Factory freeze, building/banking/auction disabled. Immediate on claim, lifted on loss.

### 6. Flag Bonuses While Holding
- +100% Metal/Energy harvest, +100% XP, +50% cave drops, +50% auto-farm speed
- +25% unit str/def, +50% bank/inv capacity, permanent +2% per 12hr hold

### 7. Research Tracking Tiers
- Tier 0 (FREE): Particle trail only. Tier 1-3: Quadrant→Zone→Region. Tier 4 VIP: Precise.

### 8. Notification System
- Toast + Modal + Banner via react-toastify + custom modal component
