## Converged design (implemented)

**Per-unit HP = strength + defense** (power-proportional; zero-power floor 10) —
an army's pool equals its total power projection. Mirrors fight exactly 2 rounds at
any tier; tanky DEF garrisons fight multi-round; overreached raids die fast. The
"incident still resolves R1" clause was dropped as erroneous: the honest outcome of
that matchup under any correct scale is a close-matchup-shaped fight, and the
Phase-1 structural fix already guarantees no annihilation DRAW. Single-seam change
(`calculateTotalHP`); the 100-round cap is retained as a safety net (structurally
unreachable under this scale — proven by re-running the old stalemate fixture:
its pool collapses 150× and dies in R1).
