## Flag 1 — CRITICAL, code-confirmed, fixed

The attack route's weight-class floor (`GARRISON_STR_RATIO = 0.6 × attackerSTR`) inflated
garrison **STR** — but the counter formula reads **DEF** (`defenderDEF − attackerSTR/2`;
`battleService.ts:381` passes `totalDEF`; the FID-20260914-002 comment mis-stated the
defender term). So the floor only padded garrison HP: every fresh-synth cell resolved in
1–2 rounds at 0–9% attacker losses, tier mismatch irrelevant — free wins everywhere.

**Fix** (`app/api/combat/attack/route.ts`): split the floor —
`GARRISON_DEF_RATIO = 0.65` drives the counter (0.15 × attackerSTR per round =
DEF_RATIO − 0.5), `GARRISON_STR_RATIO = 0.2` pads the HP pool. Post-fix: 13–15% attacker
losses at every mismatch, rounds scale with defender tier (2→6).
