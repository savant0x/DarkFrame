## Audit verdict (evidence in FID-20260914-008 §2)

respec/mastery/status), the `players.specialization` jsonb column, three route pairs

dead SQL on Postgres. Probed: 500 + `research_points: 0` + doctrine written + history
entry lost (partial apply; a retry would be rejected "already specialized" — charged with
a failing request). Fixed (Phase 0) to the pg jsonb append already used by
`referralService` — re-probed: **200, `Successfully specialized in Offensive Doctrine!`,
doctrine + history persisted.**

Also found (full table in the FID): mastery XP is client-granted and unbounded (open
exploit; no legitimate grantor), no nav entry to the page, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` lists a

## Perfection Loop record

Converged in 2 of 10 iterations, no oscillation. Loop 1 (design audit): bonuses must apply
at exactly two seams (unit cost at build time, army power at battle time) without baking
multipliers into stored units (respec-safety); mastery must be earned (wire the service's
own unit/battle counters), must amplify the doctrine bonus, and must be server-side only;
the specialized-units promise is cut from v1 (existing unit categories already map onto
the doctrines). Loop 2 (deep audit of the plan): double-apply risk against existing
flag/shrine/achievement bonus stacks → consume the central power computation, not a
parallel one; three independent cost sites → one helper; mastery XP values pinned as
economy knobs; respec comment contradiction fixed; jsonb counters via the shim's verified
dot-path `$inc`. No further actionable improvements.
