### 8.3 Latent defect fixed via honest typing (Law-6 dividend)

`getPlayerGameState` read **phantom columns**: `factories.units` and `factories.tier` do not
exist on the `factories` table (verified against `lib/db/schema/factories.ts` — the casts
`f as any` had been hiding this). Consequences: `unitCounts` was always `{}` and every
factory mapped to tier `'WEAK'`, so the tutorial's `build_unit` and `factory_capture`
CUSTOM validations could never pass. Fixed with real data: units counted from
`players.units` (`PlayerUnit[]` jsonb, quantity-aggregated by `unitType`); factory tier
derived from the documented level band (1→WEAK, 2→MEDIUM, 3+→STRONG) with a comment
recording that the table has no tier column.
