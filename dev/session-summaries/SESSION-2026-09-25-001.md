### 3.4 A probe of my own was wrong before it was a finding

Every green chain run I reported earlier in this session printed `CHAIN EXIT=0` from
`echo "CHAIN EXIT=${PIPESTATUS[0]}"` placed after `printf … | bash .githooks/pre-push … | tail`. `PIPESTATUS[0]`
is **`printf`'s** status, not the hook's — so those lines asserted the status of the thing that fed the hook.
The *conclusion* was independently true (each green run's last line is `✅ pre-push: attribution scan clean`,
which the hook only prints on success, and the refusal text is unmistakable when it fires), but the exit-code
claim was not what it appeared to be. Re-measured properly, all three values now come from the hook itself:
green → `CHAIN EXIT=0`, the drill → `HOOK EXIT=1`. Recorded in `FID-20260925-002` §2 finding 5 because the
lesson generalizes: **the check that looks like a verdict can be measuring its own input.**
